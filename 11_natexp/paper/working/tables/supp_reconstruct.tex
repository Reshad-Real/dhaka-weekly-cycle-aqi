Log: Block and daily models fitted to log concentrations; the reconstruction divides each Friday's observed block means by $e^{\beta_b}$, a residual-preserving multiplicative counterfactual, and the direct value $e^{\beta}-1$ is a geometric-mean contrast that, under the same construction, is also the ratio of the Friday total to its counterfactual. Arithmetic: The same designs fitted by Poisson pseudo-maximum likelihood to concentrations, so $e^{\beta}$ is a ratio of conditional arithmetic means and the fitted Friday total equals the observed one; block and daily models then estimate the same ratio of Friday to working-day totals under different mean models.
